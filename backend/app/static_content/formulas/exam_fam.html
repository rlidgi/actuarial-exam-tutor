1. Short-Term Insurance & Reinsurance Coverages (5–10%)

Payment R.V. (Deductible d, Limit u, Coinsurance α)Yᴸ = α·[min(X,u) − min(X,d)]
Loss Elimination RatioLER = E[X∧d] / E[X]
Per-Payment vs. Per-Loss VariableYᴾ = Yᴸ | X > d
Excess-of-Loss Reinsurer's Sharemin(max(X−r,0), m) (layer from retention r, width m)
Quota ShareInsurer retains α·X; Reinsurer pays (1−α)·X

2. Severity, Frequency & Aggregate Models (12.5–17.5%)

Severity Distributions

Common severity distributions — mean and variance
Distribution E[X] Var(X) Notes
Exponential(θ) θ θ² special case: Weibull(τ=1), Gamma(α=1)
Gamma(α,θ) αθ αθ² sum of α iid Exponential(θ) when α∈ℤ⁺

Aggregate Loss Models

Collective Risk ModelS = X₁+⋯+X_N, E[S]=E[N]E[X], Var(S)=E[N]Var(X)+Var(N)E[X]²
Individual Risk ModelS = Y₁+⋯+Yₙ, Yᵢ independent (not identical)
Normal ApproximationP(S≤s) ≈ Φ[(s−E[S])/√Var(S)]
Lognormal Approximation (Moment Matching)σ² = ln(1+Var(S)/E[S]²), μ = ln(E[S]) − σ²/2
Expected Stop-Loss PaymentE[(S−d)⁺] = E[S] − E[S∧d]
Value at RiskVaR_p(S) = inf{s : F_S(s) ≥ p}
Tail Value at RiskTVaR_p(S) = VaR_p(S) + E[(S−VaR_p(S))⁺] / (1−p)

3. Parametric Estimation (2.5–7.5%)

Likelihood — Complete DataL(θ) = Π f(xᵢ; θ)
Likelihood — Grouped DataL(θ) = Π [F(c_(j+1);θ) − F(c_j;θ)]^(n_j)
Likelihood — Left Truncation at dContribution = f(x;θ) / S(d;θ), x > d
Likelihood — Right Censoring at uContribution = S(u;θ)

5. Pricing & Reserving for Short-Term Coverages (10–15%)

Expected Loss Ratio ReserveUltimate Losses = ELR × Earned Premium
Chain-Ladder Age-to-Age Factorf_k = Σ(losses at age k+1) / Σ(losses at age k), volume-weighted
Bornhuetter-FergusonUltimate = Actual-to-date + (1 − 1/CDF)×(Expected Losses)
Permissible Loss RatioPLR = 1 − Variable Expense Ratio − Target Profit %
Loss Ratio MethodIndicated Rate Change = (Experience Loss Ratio / PLR) − 1
Pure Premium MethodIndicated Rate = Projected Pure Premium / PLR

6. Option Pricing Fundamentals (2.5–7.5%)

Call / Put PayoffCall: max(S_T−K,0) Put: max(K−S_T,0)
Binomial Risk-Neutral Probabilityp* = (e^(rh) − d) / (u − d)
Binomial Option PriceV₀ = e^(−rh)[p*·V_u + (1−p*)·V_d]
Black-Scholes Call PriceC = S₀N(d₁) − Ke^(−rT)N(d₂)
d1, d2d₁ = [ln(S₀/K) + (r+σ²/2)T] / (σ√T), d₂ = d₁ − σ√T
DeltaΔ_call = N(d₁), Δ_put = N(d₁) − 1
Put-Call ParityC − P = S₀ − Ke^(−rT)

7. Mortality Models (10–15%)

Survival Probabilityₜpₓ = S(x+t)/S(x) = l_(x+t)/l_x
Force of Mortalityμ(x) = −S′(x)/S(x), S(x) = exp[−∫₀ˣ μ(s)ds]
De Moivreμ(x) = 1/(ω−x), ₜpₓ = (ω−x−t)/(ω−x)
Constant Force (Exponential)μ(x) = μ, ₜpₓ = e^(−μt)
Gompertz / Makehamμ(x) = Bcˣ (Gompertz), μ(x) = A+Bcˣ (Makeham)
Complete / Curtate Expectatione̊ₓ = ∫₀^∞ ₜpₓ dt, eₓ = Σ_(k≥1) ₖpₓ, e̊ₓ ≈ eₓ + 0.5 (UDD)
UDD Fractional Ageₛqₓ = s·qₓ, 0≤s≤1

8. Present Value Random Variables (12.5–20%)

Whole Life Insurance (discrete)Z = v^(K_x+1), A_x = E[Z] = Σ_(k≥0) v^(k+1)·ₖpₓ·q_(x+k)
Whole Life Insurance (continuous)Z = v^(T_x), Āₓ = ∫₀^∞ vᵗ·ₜpₓ·μ(x+t) dt
Whole Life Annuity-Dueäₓ = Σ_(k≥0) vᵏ·ₖpₓ
Insurance–Annuity IdentityAₓ = 1 − d·äₓ (discrete); Āₓ = 1 − δ·āₓ (continuous)
Variance — InsuranceVar(Z) = ²Aₓ − (Aₓ)² (²Aₓ uses double the force of interest, i.e. v²)
Variance — AnnuityVar(Y) = [²Aₓ − (Aₓ)²] / d²

9. Premium & Policy Value Calculation (15–22.5%)

Future Loss R.V. (Whole Life)L₀ = v^(K_x+1) − P·ä_(K_x+1⌐)
Equivalence Principle PremiumE[L₀]=0 ⟹ P = Aₓ/äₓ = (1/äₓ) − d (whole life)
Prospective Policy ValueₜV = EPV(future benefits) − EPV(future net premiums)
Recursive Reserve Relationship(ₜV + P)(1+i) = q_(x+t)·(benefit) + p_(x+t)·ₜ₊₁V
Extra Risk — Constant Additionμ*(x) = μ(x) + c
Extra Risk — Constant Multipleq*(x) = k·q(x)
