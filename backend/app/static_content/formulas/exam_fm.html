1. Time Value of Money (5–15%)

Compound Accumulationa(t) = (1+i)ᵗ, AV = PV(1+i)ⁿ
Discount Factorv = 1/(1+i), PV = AV·vⁿ
Simple Interesta(t) = 1 + it
Effective Discount Rated = iv = 1−v = i/(1+i)
Nominal Rate Conversion1+i = (1+i⁽ᵐ⁾/m)ᵐ = (1−d⁽ᵐ⁾/m)⁻ᵐ = e^δ
Force of Interest (constant)δ = ln(1+i)
Force of Interest (variable)δ(t) = a′(t)/a(t), a(t) = exp[∫₀ᵗ δ(s)ds]
Equation of ValueΣ(inflows disc./accum. to T) = Σ(outflows disc./accum. to T)

Rate Conversion Quick-Reference

Given To find i
i⁽ᵐ⁾ i = (1+i⁽ᵐ⁾/m)ᵐ − 1
d i = d/(1−d)
d⁽ᵐ⁾ i = (1−d⁽ᵐ⁾/m)⁻ᵐ − 1
δ i = e^δ − 1

2. Annuities (20–30%)

Annuity-Immediate (PV, AV)a_n⌐ = (1−vⁿ)/i, s_n⌐ = [(1+i)ⁿ−1]/i
Annuity-Due (PV, AV)ä_n⌐ = a_n⌐(1+i) = (1−vⁿ)/d, s̈_n⌐ = s_n⌐(1+i)
Level Perpetuitiesa_∞⌐ = 1/i (immediate), ä_∞⌐ = 1/d (due)
Annuity Payable m-thlya_n⌐⁽ᵐ⁾ = (1−vⁿ)/i⁽ᵐ⁾, ä_n⌐⁽ᵐ⁾ = (1−vⁿ)/d⁽ᵐ⁾
Continuous Annuityā_n⌐ = (1−vⁿ)/δ
Deferred Annuity (m-year deferral, n-year immediate)PV = vᵐ · a_n⌐
Increasing Annuity-Immediate(Ia)_n⌐ = [ä_n⌐ − n·vⁿ] / i
Decreasing Annuity-Immediate(Da)_n⌐ = [n − a_n⌐] / i
Increasing Perpetuity-Immediate(Ia)_∞⌐ = 1/i + 1/i² = (1+i)/i²
Geometric Annuity (payments 1,(1+k),(1+k)²,…)PV = [1 − ((1+k)/(1+i))ⁿ] / (i−k), i ≠ k
Geometric PerpetuityPV = 1/(i−k), requires i > k

3. Loans (15–25%)

Level Loan PaymentPrincipal = Payment × a_n⌐
Outstanding Balance — ProspectiveB_t = Payment × a_(n−t)⌐
Outstanding Balance — RetrospectiveB_t = Principal×(1+i)ᵗ − Payment × s_t⌐
Interest & Principal SplitI_t = i × B_(t−1), P_t = Payment − I_t
Principal Repaid GrowthP_t = P_1 × (1+i)^(t−1) (level-payment loan)

4. Bonds (15–25%)

Bond PriceP = Fr × a_n⌐ + C × vⁿ
Premium / DiscountP − C = (Fr − Ci) × a_n⌐
Book Value RecursionB_t = B_(t−1)(1+i) − Fr
Premium Amortized in Period tFr − i·B_(t−1)
Discount Accumulated in Period ti·B_(t−1) − Fr

Premium vs. Discount at a Glance

Relationship Bond trades at Book value over time
r > i Premium (P > C) Amortizes down to C
r < i Discount (P < C) Accumulates up to C
r = i Par (P = C) Constant at C

5. General Cash Flows, Portfolios & ALM (20–30%)

Dollar-Weighted Return (exact)B(1+i) − ΣC_t(1+i)^(1−t) = A
Dollar-Weighted Return (approx.)i ≈ I / [B + ΣC_t(1−t)]
Time-Weighted Return1+i = Π(Value just before each CF / Value just after previous CF)
Forward Rate from Spot Rates(1+s_t)ᵗ(1+f_[t,t+k])ᵏ = (1+s_(t+k))^(t+k)
Macaulay DurationD_Mac = Σt·PV(CF_t) / ΣPV(CF_t)
Modified DurationD_mod = D_Mac / (1+i)
ConvexityC = Σt(t+1)·PV(CF_t) / [ΣPV(CF_t) × (1+i)²]
1st-Order Approx. (modified duration)ΔPV/PV ≈ −D_mod × Δi
1st-Order Approx. (Macaulay duration)ΔPV/PV ≈ −D_Mac × Δi / (1+i)
2nd-Order (Convexity-Adjusted)ΔPV/PV ≈ −D_mod×Δi + ½×C×(Δi)²

Redington Immunization — Three Conditions

# Condition
1 PV(Assets) = PV(Liabilities)
2 Duration(Assets) = Duration(Liabilities)
3 Convexity(Assets) > Convexity(Liabilities)

Immunization / Matching Techniques Compared

Technique Protects against Requires
Redington immunization Small parallel shifts in i PV, duration, convexity conditions above
Full immunization Any size shift in i Asset CF strictly before & after each liability CF ("sandwich")
Cash-flow matching / dedication Any shift in i (no reinvestment risk) Exact asset CF for every liability CF
